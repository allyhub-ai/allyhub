# Decks

## Before you start

This skill works in an AllyHub project and needs the allyhub skill (`npx skills add allyhub-ai/allyhub --skill allyhub -g -y`).

## Goal

A deck a person would take for a designer's work, in a look that is this brand's and no template's, that says what its audience must hear, and that the person can go on editing.

## Process

1. Find out who the deck is for, what it must make them do, and what the brand already looks like; research the product and decks and brands whose look fits it, and ask the person only what you cannot find.
2. Write the story first, a slide at a time, each with one thing to say.
3. Decide the look and keep it in the project as the brand's style guide, then make it the deck's own theme, fonts and layouts, so every slide and every later deck starts from it.
4. Make the slides, drawing what the story needs (the product, its numbers) as the deck's own, with what the person will want to change kept as real text and shapes.
5. Look at every slide as a picture, as a demanding art director would, and go on until nothing on it reads as a default.
6. Tell the person what only they can decide or check, such as a font their computer lacks, and keep what you learned in the project.
