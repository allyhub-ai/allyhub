# Product demos

## Goal

A product demo video in the style of Screen Studio, made from the real product, and the brand and interface kit in the project that every later video builds on.

## Process

1. Research the brand, the product and its competitors, and keep what you learn in the project: the brand (logo, colours, fonts, voice) and the product's positioning.
2. Use the product as a user would; at a login wall, ask the person to sign you in, then go on.
3. Rebuild the product's core screens the video needs as reusable UI components in the project, as close to the real product as you can, with its own logo, colours and fonts used as they are, since it is the person's own product.
4. Decide the core feature to show and how to make its value clear, write the video's script, and make the video in code from the script.
