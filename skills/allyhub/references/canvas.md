# references/canvas.md

A canvas lays a project's work out to be seen together, compared and talked over: a launch's outputs side by side, two cuts of a video, a mood board, candidates with your reasons beside them. It draws nothing of its own and exports nothing. It is a `.canvas` file (JSON Canvas, as Obsidian writes it) anywhere in the project, written by hand and listed in `allyhub.yaml` for the person to see it.

- `allyhub help canvases` lists the commands and prints where the SDK's `canvas.md` is. Read it before writing a canvas.
- **The person moves things and adds notes and edges in the same file.** Read it before you change it, and keep what they put there.
- `allyhub inspect canvas <file>` lists what it holds and where, without drawing it; `allyhub dev <file>` opens it for the person.
