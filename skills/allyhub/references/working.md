# references/working.md

- **Mark up.** In Studio a person shows what to change with **Mark up**: its row of pens at the foot of every editor (the last item of an editor's right-click menu, or ⌘⇧M, brings it back). Picking a pen takes a picture of what is on show; they circle, box, point an arrow, underline or write on it, add it to the chat, then say what to change. Studio names the button in the person's language (批改 in Chinese).
- **A note and its marks.** Their message names notes by number; each note says what was on show (the item, its version, the moment, page, heading or rows) and each mark's place in shares of the picture and what it is on: a video's layer and part, a slide's shape, a doc's block, a sheet's range, a 3D object, with the file and line where the editor knows them. An arrow means what its tip points at, and words written on the picture are part of the request. A mark that names nothing is found on the picture from its place. The picture is of that moment: a piece changed since shows something else there now.
- **Outside Studio,** the person points with their app's own annotation, over `allyhub dev`'s page where the app shows it: Claude's Browser pane has its own (a pen, boxes and text drawn on the page, sent with a picture), Codex's browser pane has Annotation Mode. A page opened in the system browser has none.
