# references/working.md

The person asks for the work in their own words, and reviews and edits the pieces in AllyHub's editors.

- **Use the product.** Use a software product the way its users do. `allyhub frames web` takes a page's screenshot with its elements' boxes.
- **Rebuild in code.** A product's interface is rebuilt in code, in place of a recording: its screens become components the project keeps, reused by every later piece.
- **Speak in the work's terms.** Tell the person what a piece shows and says, and what changed. Show code only when they ask.
- **Let them point.** In Studio a person shows you what to change with **Mark up**: its row of pens at the foot of every editor (the last item of an editor's right-click menu, or ⌘⇧M, brings it back). Picking a pen takes a picture of what is on show; they circle, box, point an arrow, underline or write on it, add it to the chat, then say what to change. When you first hand a piece over in Studio, tell them in one sentence that they can circle anything on it and say what to change. Name the button as Studio shows it in their language (批改 in Chinese).
- **Read a note by what its marks are on.** Their message names notes by number; each note says what was on show (the item, its version, the moment, page, heading or rows) and each mark's place in shares of the picture and what it is on: a video's layer and part, a slide's shape, a doc's block, a sheet's range, a 3D object, with the file and line where the editor knows them. Change that element, in its file. An arrow means what its tip points at, and words written on the picture are part of the request. Where a mark names nothing, find it on the picture from its place. The picture is of that moment: if you have changed the piece since, change what it now shows there.
- **Outside Studio,** the person points with your app's own annotation, over `allyhub dev`'s page where your app shows it: Claude's Browser pane has its own (a pen, boxes and text drawn on the page, sent to you with a picture), Codex's browser pane has Annotation Mode. They mark it up right there. Only where your app shows the page in no pane of its own (a link opened in the system browser), ask for the moment, the slide, the heading or the cell, and mention that Studio has Mark up, on the desktop or at app.allyhub.com.
- **To see or operate a web page,** use the browser of the app you run in (Studio's `playwright-cli`, Codex's `@Browser` or `@Chrome`, your host's own tools). AllyHub's headless Chrome is for drawing, and `frames web` is for a screenshot. With no browser of your own, use Microsoft's `playwright-cli` (`npx @playwright/cli`).
