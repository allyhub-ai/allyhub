# references/x.md

What will be published on X is written in X's own model, so what X counts and refuses is checked before it goes:

- **An X post** (`x-post`): a post, a thread or a long post, with its pictures, video and poll.
- **An X article** (`x-article`): a long piece in X's article blocks, with its title and cover.

Their pictures and videos can be the project's own items, an image's boards or a video, made when the post or article is rendered.

- `allyhub help x` lists the commands and prints where the SDK's `x.md` is. Read it before writing a post or an article.
- `allyhub frames` draws a post or an article as X shows it once published, and `allyhub inspect` gives what X counts. Look with them before handing it over.
- **Publishing to X is not built.** `allyhub render` writes the requests X's API takes, or a post's text to paste.
