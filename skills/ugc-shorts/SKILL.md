# UGC shorts

## Goal

One feature in a short vertical video that looks filmed on a phone in a room, opening on a hook in a person's words, made for sharing and testing hooks, never promised to go viral.

## Process

1. Use the feature in the real product, and rebuild its screens as UI components, or reuse those the project has.
2. Write a few hooks and the script; a hook may be said by an AI presenter made with AllyHub's video models (`video-grok-imagine-1-5-lite` does it well).
3. Make the short in code, in a room built in 3D with the feature on a screen in it.
